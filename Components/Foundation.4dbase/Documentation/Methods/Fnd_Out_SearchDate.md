These are understood: a full date in the system's short format or ISO form; the localised words for today and yesterday; a bare number, which is that day of the current month; and a day and month typed in the system's order, such as 4/11 or 11/4, which is that day of the current year.Attributes: Shared, Invisible, Preemptive indifferent| Parameter | Type |  | Description || --- | --- | --- | --- ||searchText|Text|➡️|The text typed by the user||Result|Date|⬅️|The date, or the blank date !00-00-00! when the text is not one|
