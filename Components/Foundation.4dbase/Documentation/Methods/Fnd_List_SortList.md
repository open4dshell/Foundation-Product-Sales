Call it from the hook that adds them (Fnd\_Hook\_Shell\_Administration, Fnd\_Hook\_Shell\_Print or Fnd\_Hook\_Shell\_SpecialFunctions). Pass "asc" for A to Z, "desc" for Z to A, or "none" to show them in the order they were added, which is the default.Attributes: Shared, Invisible, Preemptive indifferent| Parameter | Type |  | Description || --- | --- | --- | --- ||order|Text|➡️|"asc", "desc" or "none"|
